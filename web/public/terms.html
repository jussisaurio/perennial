Last updated: 12 October 2026

These terms apply when you use Perennial, an app for learning words in a new language. Perennial is run by Jussi Saurio, a private person in Finland (“we”). When you use the app, you agree to these terms. Please also read our privacy policy.

The service

  • Perennial is free to use. You can start without an account.
  • If you add your email address, you can sign in with a code and use your progress on other devices.
  • An anonymous account (without email) is deleted after 60 days with no activity. Add your email to keep your progress.
  • There are limits on how much you can store (for example, words and reviews) and how many requests you can send. They are high enough for normal learning.
  • We can change, add or remove features. We try to keep your progress safe when we do this.

Perennial AI

  • Perennial AI makes cards from your own texts and writes new example sentences. It needs an account with an email address.
  • You get a number of AI words each month. Unused AI words do not carry over to the next month.
  • We can pause Perennial AI for everyone when it reaches our monthly cost limit. It starts again the next month.
  • An AI model writes this content. It can make mistakes. Check important meanings in a dictionary.
  • Send only text that you have the right to use. Do not send personal or secret information.

You give us permission to store and process them only to run the service for you (for example, to sync them and to send them to the AI model when you ask for it).

You can stop at any time. To delete your account and your data, go to Settings → Your data → “Delete my account”. Download your data first if you want a copy.
