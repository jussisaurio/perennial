Perennial is an app for learning words in a new language. This page tells you what data we keep, why we keep it, who else sees it, and how you can delete it. We wrote it in simple English. If something is not clear, write to us (see Contact).

The short version

  • We keep your learning progress so that you can use it on all your devices.
  • We keep your email address only if you add it, so that you can sign in.
  • We do not show ads. We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising.
  • You can download your data and delete your account in Settings.

You can start without an account. In that case we make an anonymous account with no email address. If you add your email later, we move your progress into that account.

On your device

The app keeps a copy of your learning data on your device, so it works offline. It also keeps changes that are not sent yet, the course content, and a few preferences (theme, sound, and graphics quality). These preferences stay on your device and are not sent to us. To remove this data, sign out and clear the site data in your browser.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie: the session cookie that keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who else processes your data

We use these services to run Perennial. Each one gets only the data it needs.

  • Railway runs our server and keeps its logs.
  • PlanetScale runs our database (Postgres), where your account and learning data are stored.
  • Resend sends the email with your sign-in code. It gets your email address and the code.
  • Anthropic runs the AI model for Perennial AI. It gets only the text that you choose to send: the words you tap, the sentences they come from, the words you ask for new sentences for, and a list of words you already know. It does not get your email address or your name. We use Perennial AI only when you start it. Anonymous learners cannot use it.
  • Sentry (only if we turn it on) gets error reports from our server and from the app when something breaks. A report contains technical details (the error, the page or request address, your browser and device type), and your user id. Before a report leaves our server, we remove cookies, sign-in codes, the content of requests, and everything about you except your user id. It does not contain your email address.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile (only if we turn it on) checks that a person, not a program, is starting a new account or asking for a sign-in code. To do this, Cloudflare gets your IP address and technical details from your browser. It does not get your email address or your learning data.
  • PostHog (only if we turn it on) gets a few named events, so that we can see if the app works for people: a course started (language and minutes per day), a session finished (how many answers and new words), an email added, the garden opened, and words added. Each event has your user id, not your email address. PostHog does not set cookies here, does not record your screen, and does not collect clicks automatically. If your browser sends “Do Not Track”, the app sends no events.

How long we keep data

  • Anonymous accounts (no email): deleted after 60 days with no activity, with all their data.
  • Accounts with an email: kept until you delete the account.
  • AI request logs: deleted after 180 days.
  • Abuse protection: the counters for an IP address end after one hour at most. The scrambled email addresses with the times of sign-in codes are deleted after 2 days.
  • Sign-in codes work for 10 minutes and are deleted after they expire. Sessions end after 90 days without use and are then deleted.
  • Backups of the database are kept for [N] days. Deleted data leaves the backups when they expire.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Settings → Your data → “Download all my data (JSON)”. The file has your data for the language you are learning now; switch the course to download another language.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Your data → “Delete my account”. This deletes your account and all your learning data on our server at once. It cannot be undone.
  • Correct your data: you can edit or delete your own words in the app. For other changes, write to us.
