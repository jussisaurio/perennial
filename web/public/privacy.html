Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Perennial is an app for learning words in a new language. It is run by Jussi Saurio, a private person in Finland (“we”). This page tells you what data we keep, why we keep it, who else sees it, and how you can delete it. We wrote it in simple English. If something is not clear, write to us (see Contact).

The short version

  • We keep your learning progress so that you can use it on all your devices.
  • We keep your email address only if you add it, so that you can sign in.
  • We do not show ads. We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising.
  • You can download your data and delete your account in Settings.

You can start without an account. In that case we make an anonymous account with no email address. If you add your email later, we move your progress into that account.

On your device

The app keeps a copy of your learning data on your device, so it works offline. It also keeps changes that are not sent yet, the course content, and a few preferences (theme, sound, and graphics quality). These preferences stay on your device and are not sent to us. To remove this data, sign out and clear the site data in your browser.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie: the session cookie that keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who else processes your data

We use these services to run Perennial. Each one gets only the data it needs.

  • Railway runs our server and keeps its logs. Location: Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
  • PlanetScale runs our database (Postgres), where your account and learning data are stored. Location: the Netherlands (on Google Cloud).
  • Resend sends the email with your sign-in code. It gets your email address and the code. Location: Ireland (on Amazon Web Services).
  • Anthropic runs the AI model for Perennial AI. It gets only the text that you choose to send: the words you tap, the sentences they come from, the words you ask for new sentences for, and a list of words you already know. It does not get your email address or your name. We use Perennial AI only when you start it. Anonymous learners cannot use it. Location: the United States.
  • Sentry: we use Sentry to find errors. It gets error reports from our server and from the app when something breaks. A report contains technical details (the error, the page or request address, your browser and device type) and your user id. It does not contain your email address or your IP address. Before a report leaves our server, we remove cookies, sign-in codes, the content of requests, and everything about you except your user id. When something breaks in the app, Sentry also keeps a short replay of the screen around that moment. In the replay, all text and everything you typed is hidden, and images are left out. So it shows where things went wrong, but not what you read or wrote. Sentry also gets warnings and errors from the app and the server, and timing data for some requests. These contain ids and timings, not your content. Location: Germany (Sentry’s EU region).
  • Cloudflare sits in front of our website. It passes the traffic between your browser and our server and protects the site from attacks, so it sees your IP address and the requests your browser sends. Location: worldwide, often in a data center near you.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile (only if we turn it on) checks that a person, not a program, is starting a new account or asking for a sign-in code. To do this, Cloudflare gets your IP address and technical details from your browser. It does not get your email address or your learning data.
  • PostHog: we use PostHog to count a few named events, so that we can see if the app works for people: a course started (language and minutes per day), a session finished (how many answers and new words), an email added, the garden opened, and words added. Each event has your user id, not your email address. PostHog does not store your IP address or where you are, does not set cookies here, does not record your screen, and does not collect clicks automatically. If your browser sends “Do Not Track”, the app sends no events. Location: Frankfurt, Germany (PostHog’s EU cloud).

All these companies are based in the United States, even when they keep the data in the EU. This means that some data can go to the United States. Anthropic and Cloudflare also use your data there. To protect your data when it leaves the EU:

  • Railway, Sentry, PostHog and Cloudflare take part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The European Commission has decided that this framework gives adequate protection.
  • For PlanetScale, Anthropic and Resend, we use the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission, which are part of their data processing terms.

Why we are allowed to use your data

The GDPR (the EU data protection law) lets us use your data for these reasons:

  • To give you the service you asked for (GDPR article 6(1)(b)): your learning data, your email address, your session, and Perennial AI.
  • Our legitimate interest (GDPR article 6(1)(f)) in keeping the service safe, working and affordable: abuse protection, server logs, error reports, usage events, and the AI limits. We keep this data small, and you can object to it (see below).

How long we keep data

  • Anonymous accounts (no email): deleted after 60 days with no activity, with all their data.
  • Accounts with an email: kept until you delete the account.
  • AI request logs: deleted after 180 days.
  • IP counters end one hour after your last request at most, and are deleted within a day.
  • Sign-in code log: deleted after 2 days.
  • Server logs (Railway): deleted after 7 days.
  • Error reports (Sentry): deleted after 30 days.
  • Usage events (PostHog): deleted after 1 year.
  • When you delete your account, the server logs, error reports and usage events are not deleted at once. They have only your user id, not your email address, and they are deleted when their time runs out.
  • Sign-in codes work for 10 minutes and are deleted after they expire. Sessions end after 90 days without use and are then deleted.
  • Backups of the database are made every 12 hours and kept for 2 days. Deleted data leaves the backups when they expire.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Settings → Your data → “Download all my data (JSON)”. The file has your data for the language you are learning now; switch the course to download another language.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Your data → “Delete my account”. This deletes your account and all your learning data on our server at once. It cannot be undone.
  • Correct your data: you can edit or delete your own words in the app. For other changes, write to us.
  • Your other rights: under the GDPR, you can ask us for a copy of your data, and ask us to correct it, delete it, or stop or limit how we use it. You can also object when we use your data because of our legitimate interest. Write to us (see Contact). We answer within one month.
  • Complain: if you think we use your data in a wrong way, you can complain to a data protection authority. In Finland, this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto). You can also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.
