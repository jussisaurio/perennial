# decks/de/source/0001.json: findings

Author: claude-agent:deck-pipeline-2026-10-11
Proofreader: claude-agent:de-proofreader-2026-10-11

- **mit**: "Ich komme mit dir." Not natural. A native speaker says "Ich komme mit." (separable *mitkommen*) or "Ich gehe mit dir."; the English phrasing shows through. It also repeats the "come with you/us" context of *wir* ("Kommst du mit uns?"). Fix: replace with text "Ich fahre mit dem Bus.", translation "I'm taking the bus.", cloze "mit".
- **sich**: gloss "himself; herself; themselves". None of the three sentences uses *sich* in that sense. All are inherent reflexives (*sich freuen*, *sich setzen*, *sich fühlen*) where English has no "-self". Fix: gloss "oneself (reflexive: himself, herself, itself, themselves)".
- **sich**: the same problem from the sentence side. No sentence shows the glossed meaning. Fix: add the sentence text "Er sieht sich im Spiegel.", translation "He sees himself in the mirror.", cloze "sich".
- **auf**: "Ich warte auf dich." Not the glossed sense (on; onto): *warten auf* = "wait for". With "Auf Wiedersehen!" (a fixed phrase), two of three sentences do not show the gloss, and the "onto" sense (accusative) has no sentence. Fix: replace with text "Die Katze springt auf das Bett.", translation "The cat jumps onto the bed.", cloze "auf". Add notes "warten auf = to wait for. Auf Wiedersehen! = Goodbye!" to explain the fixed phrase.
