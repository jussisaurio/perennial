# The story in English, as it stands (2026-11-14)

The notes and diary pages in the order a learner finds them, with the garden's thoughts between them. It is the English of Writing batch 1 (#936, Part A, with the batch's drafts and arc 2's changes). The judges in docs/judges/story get the part of this that comes before their line, as "the story so far". The harness cuts it at a line `## <id>`.

A section's "Thoughts after it" line is left out when the judge's line comes right after that section, because it may hold the line being judged.

The garden: a learner walks a small 3D garden inside a language app. The notes hang in the shed. The diary pages lie on a stone on a hill past the stile. A thought shows when the learner taps a thing.

## n01
This is the house. The garden isn't mine. It's hers.
I don't like snails.
She says that's no reason.

Thoughts after it: bench "It's for two." Stool on the hill "It's for one."

## n02
She tells me the name of a flower.
I say it too.
Tomorrow I won't know it.
The flower will.

## n03
Today I'm in the garden all day. (One word is crossed out and written again, right: his own mistake.)
The plants don't talk.
Me neither. Yet.

Thoughts after it: pond "There's a fish. Or two." Grass "More and more. Only that."

## n04
There's a little door in the hedge. Not for me.
It's for Migas. Migas eats our bread
and never says sorry.
The cat looks at Migas. Migas looks at the cat.
Nobody does anything.

## n05
Every evening, the same walk: to the end of the garden.
She jumps the stile without looking.
Me, slowly, carefully.
She says the brambles are faster than me.

(The learner stands before brambles that have grown over the stile.)
Thoughts after it: stone "Here before anyone. It knows things." Cherry "A week of party. Then it waits a year." Hills "Nobody comes from there." Sunflower "It looks at the sun. Not at you."

## n06
Her wooden chair is too low. I tell her every morning.
She says it's comfortable, and the problem is my knees.
Maybe.
But the chair is hers.

Thoughts after it: the frog, tapped at dusk, croaks, then says "You again?" and "I have no more words." (always its last line). Bed "Two sides. Hers wins." Hedge "The dog has a lot to say."

## n07
Today, a long sentence, whole, no mistakes.
She laughed until she cried.
Not at the sentence: I'd said it to the neighbour's dog.

Thoughts after it: sundial "No sun, no time."

## n08
Past the brambles and the stile, a hill.
From the top, at midnight: the far light,
a yellow light across the valley.
It never goes out, not even in winter.
She doesn't ask.

(The way over the stile opens.)
Thoughts after it: stool "Worn smooth. Many nights." Hills "From there, this looks small." Sunflower "She knows its name."

## n09
The far light: someone who doesn't talk to me. Pride, on both sides.
A silly argument, eleven winters ago.
Back then I couldn't say sorry in any language.
Now, in two. Courage, in none.

## p1
A snail on my stool again.
I don't like snails.
She keeps saying that's no reason.

Thoughts after it: pond "The fish watch who's coming." Lantern "Every night, friends. Always the same ones."

## d1
Tonight, the far light, as always.
She brings me up a coffee.
She doesn't ask.

## p2
Wind on the hill. I said nothing.

## d2
Tonight, the far light, as always.
She brings me up a coffee and something for the wind.
She doesn't ask. She stays.

Thoughts after it: moon "On time again."

## p3
She says my stool is too low.
Maybe.

## d3
Tonight, the far light, as always.
I bring my own coffee up.
Nobody asks.

Thoughts after it: sunflower "She looked at it every morning." Bed "Her side. Still winning." Sundial "Nobody checks the time anymore."

## n10
I watered your side of the garden too.

Thoughts after it: bench "Someone sat here every evening." Hedge "Every morning, less water."

## n11
Two cups on the table. One stays empty.
I leave it by the little door, with water. For Migas.
Rayo the snail listens to me. He never says no.

## p4
Last night I made a signal with the light. Just one.
Nobody answered.

## n12
Last night I made two signals with the light.
The far light answered with two.
Nobody has crossed the valley. Yet.

Thoughts after it: frog "No kisses, please. Those stories aren't true." Hills "Over there, someone isn't sleeping either." Frog "I'm not a prince."

## n13
Now the garden is yours.
Water both sides.
Leave water for Migas.
At night, two signals to the far light. It always answers.
Don't like snails? That's no reason.
